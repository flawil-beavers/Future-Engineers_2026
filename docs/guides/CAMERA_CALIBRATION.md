# Camera calibration

Use this bench procedure to check distance and sign height with the camera in its driving position.

1. **Set up a reference.** Keep the camera fixed in its driving position and the car on a level mat under representative lighting. Put one sign straight ahead with its top and base visible. Measure horizontally from the lens to the sign’s near face. We used **350, 500 and 700 mm** for our reference set.
2. **Read the estimates.** Keep SW1 LOW. Connect via USB, open the serial monitor at **115200 baud** and send `c0`. In the `[CAM CAL]` output, check `color` and `production_valid=yes`. Compare `range_est_mm` with your tape measure and the height after the slash in `full_top_y/height_mm` with the actual sign height. Record `max_y`, the image row of the sign’s base, for each distance. Stop diagnostics with `z`.
3. **Refit if the ranges are consistently off.** For each distance, use a representative `max_y` from clear frames. Fit a straight line with `1 / distance_mm` on the horizontal axis and `max_y` on the vertical axis. Its intercept gives `OBSTACLE_CAMERA_GROUND_HORIZON_Y` and its slope gives `OBSTACLE_CAMERA_GROUND_RANGE_SCALE_MM_PX`. Enter the fitted values in `config.h`, then rebuild and upload M7. [Camera settings](../../include/config.h#L1377-L1381)
4. **Recheck with both colours.** Verify the range at placements you did not use for the fit. For the modified-sign option, compare estimates for **50, 100 and 150 mm** signs with the firmware’s short, normal and tall ranges. If the range is accurate and height is consistently off, keep the ground fit fixed and adjust `OBSTACLE_CAMERA_FOCAL_Y_PX` to minimise squared height errors on known red signs. Rebuild M7 and check the result on green signs.
