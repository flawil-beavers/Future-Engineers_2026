large wall correction. Log 510 changed Y by -25.4 mm, then passed a marker

remaining line error is corrected before scanning through the markers.

little settling room, bounded reversals between approximately X=900 and 1080 mm

20/25/20/90 mm with **28/34/28/80 mm**. The short turns establish more outward
yaw before the long forward sweep, with adequate rear clearance. Final alignment

diagnostic target changes from 155 to 170 mm.

not a physical contact sensor or a guarantee against unknown pose error.
