Source-level chain:

1. A long polling gap invalidates gyro health until a fresh valid quaternion.
2. If that loop has no valid quaternion,main executes stop(false) and returns.
   stop(false) disables motor AND servo writes.
3. On fresh gyro reports,main resumes the normal route. set_speed restarts the
   motor,while set_steering only changes the requested angle;it does NOT enable
   servo writes. steer() silently returns while servo_disabled remains true.
4. The vehicle can therefore drive with the last physical steering,which was
   centered for the stationary observation,instead of tracking the GREEN bypass.
