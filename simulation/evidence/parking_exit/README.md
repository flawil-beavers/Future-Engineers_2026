# Parking-exit footprint photographs

These top-down photographs were captured on 2026-08-27 and used by the
parking-exit swept-footprint model. A ruler and field markings provide scale.

| File | Steering state shown |
| --- | --- |
| `PXL_20260827_113122609.MP.jpg` | Wheels approximately straight. |
| `PXL_20260827_113154265.MP.jpg` | Front wheels at one manually positioned full-lock extreme. |
| `PXL_20260827_113214902.MP.jpg` | Front wheels at the opposite manually positioned full-lock extreme. |

The photographs support the multi-polygon footprint described in
`simulation/PARKING_EXIT_PATH_SIMULATION.md`: central chassis, narrow
front/rear protrusions, rear wheels, and independently rotated front wheels.
They show why a solid maximum-width rectangle is too pessimistic around the
parking pieces.

These images are measurement evidence, not calibrated photogrammetry. The
camera is not guaranteed perfectly perpendicular, perspective is present, the
wheel angles were positioned manually, and the ruler/field print can introduce
scale error. Re-capture and revalidate the model after changes to the chassis,
wheels, steering linkage, wiring envelope, or mounted components.
