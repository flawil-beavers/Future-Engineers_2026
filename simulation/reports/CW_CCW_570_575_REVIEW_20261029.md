## Scoped corrections

1. Unresolved CW short-start scouts may extend their existing reverse arc
   toward an18deg central view. Total extra is capped at30mm. Existing full
   parking footprint/all possible initial inner pillars, heading+/-1deg,
   5mm footprint reserve and8mm stopping allowance remain in preflight.
   Original encoder baseline is retained; forward return uses the full
   measured reverse travel. Resolved scouts retain their original motion.
2. Unresolved CCW front-place views get one checked short **forward** arc,
   steering-50, toward10deg target bearing after at least400ms observation.
   Maximum35mm, normal entry speed, steering settle, braking hold, direction,
   travel and2500ms drive guards. Both wheel directions are checked against
   the rails, plus all three possible initial inner pillars and the same
   reserves. A further reverse arc was rejected during analysis because its
   front-wheel sweep enters the rail at some recorded poses. This rejected
   option is not part of the firmware. Already-resolved views do not move.
3. Extra corner arcs now use `pursuitCurvatureForServo`, the shared calibrated
   CAD mapping. Existing distances, clearance and tracking thresholds stay.
4. Final scan localization waits for two distinct fresh accepted stationary
   outer-wall frames after the brake hold, stable within8mm and within the
   existing25mm pose-correction bound. Edge measurements still register X;
   a fresh stopped wall registers Y. Invalid/old/duplicate frames cannot
   supply the reference. No valid pair within2500ms gives a bounded stopped
   refusal. Capture and entry collision gates remain active.

No camera mounting, colour thresholds, seat-vote requirements, normal lap
speeds or ordinary successful lap route were changed. Model success does not
establish physical reliability: images, lag, tire slip and mounting error
remain unmodeled.
